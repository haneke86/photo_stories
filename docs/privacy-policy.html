MacPhotoTrips

Privacy Policy
Effective date: February 24, 2026

The short version: Your photos never leave your device. We only send city names and dates to generate AI stories. We do not track you, show ads, or sell data. Period.

MacPhotoTrips is built by an independent developer who cares deeply about privacy. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles, where it goes, and how you can control it.

1. What We Collect

On Your Device Only

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your device. It is never uploaded to any server.

  • Photo location metadata — latitude, longitude, and date extracted from your photo library via Apple's PhotoKit framework. We read EXIF location data only; we never access the photo images themselves.
  • Travel timeline — a structured file (timeline.json) containing detected trips, cities, and date ranges.
  • Story cache — AI-generated trip narratives, cached locally for offline access.
  • Chat history — your conversations with the AI assistant, stored in a local file.
  • Geocoding cache — reverse-geocoded place names cached via SwiftData to reduce network requests.

Sent to Our Server

When you use AI features (trip stories or chat), the following is sent to our server:

  • City names and date ranges — for example, "Istanbul, June 12-18, 2024." This is the minimum information needed to generate a travel narrative.
  • Chat messages — the text you type in the chat interface.

Authentication

When you sign in with Apple, the following is stored in AWS Cognito:

  • Apple subject ID — a stable, anonymous identifier provided by Apple.
  • Email address — the email provided by Apple (which may be a private relay address if you chose to hide your email).

Usage Counters

We store simple usage counters in our database to enforce monthly limits:

  • Number of stories generated this month.
  • Number of chat messages sent this month.

That is everything. No analytics. No device fingerprints. No advertising identifiers. No behavioral tracking.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data Purpose Where
Photo location metadata Detect trips and build your travel timeline Device
City names + dates Generate AI trip narratives and power chat Server
Apple sign-in credentials Authenticate you and manage your account Server
Usage counters Enforce monthly feature limits Server

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than what is described above.

3. Data Storage and Security

On Device

All on-device data (timeline, story cache, chat history, geocoding cache) is stored in your app's sandboxed container using standard iOS storage mechanisms. This data is protected by your device's passcode and iOS data protection.

On Our Servers

Server-side data is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the US East (N. Virginia) region:

  • Authentication — managed by AWS Cognito with industry-standard security practices.
  • Usage counters — stored in AWS DynamoDB.
  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

We do not store your city names, dates, chat messages, or generated stories on our servers. These are processed in real-time by the AI and returned to your device, where they are cached locally.

We do not integrate any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporters, or other third-party tracking tools. The only frameworks used are Apple's own (PhotoKit, CoreLocation, AuthenticationServices) and the AWS SDK for authentication.

5. Your Photos

Your photos never leave your device. MacPhotoTrips reads only the location metadata (latitude, longitude, and date) embedded in your photos via Apple's PhotoKit API. The app never accesses, processes, or transmits the photo images themselves.

Location data is read from photo EXIF metadata through PhotoKit, not from your device's GPS. This data is used solely for trip detection and on-device geocoding.

6. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated server-side data at any time by contacting us at privacy@macphoto.app.

Upon receiving your request:

  • Your account enters a 30-day soft-delete period during which you can change your mind and restore it.
  • After 30 days, all server-side data is permanently purged, including your Cognito user record and all DynamoDB entries (usage counters).
  • On-device data (timeline, stories, chat history) remains on your device until you uninstall the app.

7. Data Retention

  • On-device data — retained until you uninstall the app or manually clear it.
  • Server-side authentication — retained until you delete your account.
  • Usage counters — reset monthly; deleted upon account deletion.
  • AI request content — not retained on our servers. Processed in real-time and discarded.

8. Children's Privacy

MacPhotoTrips is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@macphoto.app and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by other appropriate means before the changes take effect. The "Effective date" at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

Continued use of MacPhotoTrips after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
